Instructions for registration: if you are a voter you can register and use the facilities of this website. Follow these steps:

1. Fill in your card details and the other required information to complete the registration.

2. Enter the activation code received on your mobile or email.

3. Download the NID Wallet app on your mobile, complete face verification, and log in to your profile.
