This is a notice of the National Identity Registration Wing of the Bangladesh Election Commission. It says that from 1 September 2015 (17 Bhadra 1422) the following fees apply for NID renewal, issuing a new NID after loss or damage, correction, and data correction. This is a 2015 notice, so the current fees may be different and the Election Commission's latest notice must be checked to confirm them.

1. NID renewal: an NID is valid for 15 years from the date of issue. Before or after it expires, every citizen must apply to the Commission by the set method and fee. Fee: normal (30 working days) 100 taka; urgent (7 working days) 150 taka.

2. New NID after loss or damage: a GD must be filed at any police station quoting the NID number or voter number, and the application is made on the set form with the original GD attached. Fee: (a) first application: normal 200 taka, urgent 300 taka; (b) second application: normal 300 taka, urgent 500 taka; (c) any later application: normal 500 taka, urgent 1000 taka.

3. NID correction: correcting the information every citizen gave in Form-2 at registration, namely name in Bangla and English, father's name, mother's name, date of birth, address, blood group and signature, counts as NID correction. Fee: (a) first application 200 taka; (b) second application 300 taka; (c) any later application 400 taka.

4. Data correction: correcting any information other than what is listed in Form-2 counts as data correction. Fee: (a) first application 100 taka; (b) second application 200 taka; (c) any later application 300 taka.

How to pay: the fees can be paid by treasury challan, or by pay order or bank draft (including VAT) in favour of "Secretary, Election Commission Secretariat". For treasury challan, the fee is deposited at any branch of Bangladesh Bank and at the treasury branches of Sonali Bank under the NID fee economic code, together with 15 percent value added tax under the VAT economic code. The notice lists these economic codes and the commissionerate codes.
