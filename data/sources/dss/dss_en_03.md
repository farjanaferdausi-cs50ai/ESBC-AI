This is an English translation, prepared by the project author, of the official Bengali page at the URL above. The Bengali page is the authoritative text.

The page was last updated on 30 August 2026. The programme is run by the Department of Social Services under the Integrated Policy for the Livelihood Improvement of Disadvantaged Communities 2025.

Who counts as a disadvantaged community: under the policy, tea workers, Hijra people, Bede people and various other disadvantaged communities (for example fishermen, barbers, potters, blacksmiths, weavers, washermen and cobblers; the long list is on the page), plus any community the government declares disadvantaged from time to time.

Programmes and rates:
(a) Special allowance: 650 taka per month.
(b) Education stipend for school-going students, per month: primary level 700 taka, secondary level 800 taka, higher secondary level 1000 taka, higher education 1200 taka.
(c) Training and post-training financial support: after successfully finishing training, a one-time grant of 50,000 taka per person on the committee's recommendation (or at the rate the government sets).

How selection and payment work: applications are taken online through the Department of Social Services MIS software or directly. Where applicable the poverty PMT score is checked. A list of eligible people is made and money is paid directly (G2P) to mobile or bank accounts by priority according to the allocation. Complaints are also received and resolved through the system.
