The allowance for widows and women abandoned by their husbands is a programme of the Department of Social Services.

Allowance rate (fiscal year 2026-27): 700 taka per person per month, for the whole year. For that year the programme has an allocation for 30 lakh (3 million) people.

How it is paid: directly (G2P) through Nagad, bKash, Upay, mCash, uCash and agent banking.

Definition: a widow is a woman whose husband is dead. An abandoned wife (husband-neglected woman) is a woman who is divorced by her husband, or who for any other reason has been deprived of her husband's maintenance or out of contact with him for at least 2 years.

Eligibility conditions:
1. Must be a permanent citizen of Bangladesh and a permanent resident of the area concerned.
2. Must have a national ID card.
3. Must be older than 18 years.
4. Average yearly income must not be more than 45,000 taka.
5. Must be selected by the selection committee.

Priority: a woman who is destitute and helpless, has 2 children under 18 and is ill; a woman who is completely unable to work; the destitute, displaced and landless; a widow or abandoned wife with young children; a woman separated from her family or without children; and the oldest applicants.

Not eligible:
1. A government employee who receives a pension.
2. A destitute woman who holds a VGD card.
3. A person who regularly receives another government grant or allowance.
4. A person who regularly receives a financial grant or allowance from a private organisation or welfare institution.
5. A woman who has remarried or started living with her husband again.

Widows and abandoned wives who reach the age for the old age allowance can be moved to the old age allowance programme.
