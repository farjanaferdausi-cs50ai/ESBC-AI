This is an English translation, prepared by the project author, of the official Bengali page at the URL above. The Bengali page is the authoritative text.

The page was last updated on 18 October 2026. The programme is run by the Ministry of Social Welfare through the Department of Social Services.

Support amount: from fiscal year 2026-27 each finally selected patient receives a one-time payment of 100,000 taka (one lakh taka).

Diseases covered: cancer (all types diagnosed by a specialist); chronic kidney disease (needing regular dialysis or transplant, or already transplanted); liver cirrhosis; paralysis (caused by stroke or neurological reasons, or disability from an accident); heart disease (congenital or other heart disease needing an interventional procedure or surgery); thalassemia; leukemia.

How to apply: the patient, or a family member, guardian or legal guardian on the patient's behalf, can apply online. The signed printed copy of the application with the required documents, a photocopy of the patient's latest prescription and attested copies of the test reports must be submitted. Where to submit (as applicable): the upazila social services office, the urban social services office, the hospital social services office, the hospital authority, the Department of Social Services, or the Ministry of Social Welfare. The page has the application form (PDF). The form with the documents and attestation letters must be filled in and 2 copies submitted to your own upazila or urban social services office.

Required documents:
a) Photocopy of the national ID card of the patient and, where applicable, the applicant or guardian.
b) Photocopy of the birth registration certificate for a minor patient.
c) One passport-size photo of the patient and, where applicable, the applicant or guardian.
d) Nominee information (mandatory).
e) Bank account information of the patient, applicant or guardian (mandatory).
f) Photocopy of the cover page of the bank cheque book.
g) Photocopy of the doctor's latest prescription.
h) Attested copy of the test report that diagnoses the disease.

Test report rules: the report must be not more than one year old and an attested photocopy must be submitted. A photocopy of the film, plate or image can be attached. The original need not be attached. By disease:
Cancer: tissue diagnostic report, histopathology, cytopathology, bone marrow or biopsy report.
Kidney disease: blood urea and creatinine report with the dialysis or transplant report, and a certificate from the doctor of the hospital or service centre proving regular dialysis.
Liver cirrhosis: liver ultrasonogram and FibroScan report with other reports.
Paralysis: MRI or CT scan report, or a photo of the patient as proof.
Heart disease: angiogram or echocardiogram report with other reports.
Thalassemia and leukemia: haemoglobin electrophoresis or other applicable test report.

Patient selection criteria:
a) Must be a permanent citizen of Bangladesh.
b) Poor and low-income patients get priority (family income and expenses are considered).
c) Patients who need urgent treatment and are at risk of life get priority.
d) Disabled people, children, women and elderly patients get preference.
e) The committee can cancel an application if false information or forged documents are given, if another similar government benefit is received, or if the person declines the support after being listed.
f) No applicant can receive support more than once in the same fiscal year or more than 3 times in a lifetime.
