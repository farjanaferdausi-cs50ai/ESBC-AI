This is an English translation, prepared by the project author, of the official Bengali page at the URL above. The Bengali page is the authoritative text.

The old age allowance is a programme of the Department of Social Services (DSS) for elderly people who are poor and have low income or are unable to earn. The page has a link for online application.

Allowance rate (fiscal year 2025-26): 650 taka per person per month.

How it is paid: beneficiaries receive the allowance through mobile financial services (Nagad, bKash), agent banking, or a bank branch, as they choose.

Eligibility conditions:
(1) The applicant must be a permanent resident of the area concerned.
(2) The applicant must have a birth registration or a national ID number.
(3) Age must be at least 65 years for men and at least 62 years for women (the age set by the government from time to time must be considered).
(4) The applicant's average yearly income must not be more than 45,000 taka.
(5) The applicant must be selected by the selection committee.
To determine age, the national ID card, birth registration certificate, or SSC (or equivalent) certificate is considered. If there is a dispute, the committee's decision is final.

Priority: the oldest person; a person who is completely unable to work; the destitute, displaced and landless; widows, divorced women, widowers, childless people and people separated from their family. A person owning 0.5 acre of land or less, not counting the homestead, is treated as landless.

Not eligible:
(1) A government employee who receives a pension.
(2) A destitute woman who holds a VGD card.
(3) A person who regularly receives another government grant or allowance.
(4) A person who regularly receives a financial grant or allowance from a private organisation or welfare institution.
