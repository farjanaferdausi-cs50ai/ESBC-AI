Documents needed to be carried while enrolment at passport offices (the page states: Last updated 7 May 2025).

Required documents:
1. Printed application summary including appointment (if any).
2. Identification documents (NID card or birth certificate, original).
3. Payment slip for offline payment only.
4. Previous passport (if any).
5. GO/NOC for government service holders (as applicable).
6. Printed application form.
Further necessity of documents depends on the nature of the application and corrections (if any).

Application steps

Step 1: Check availability of e-Passport in your region. Select whether you are applying from Bangladesh, select the district of your present address and the police station nearest to your present address, and the page shows the regional passport office where you can enrol.

Step 2: Fill in your e-Passport application online.

Step 3: Pay passport fees. The page links to the fee details and the bank list.

Step 4: Visit your passport office for biometric enrolment. Make sure you have all required documents with you when you visit the passport office.

Step 5: Collect your e-Passport at the passport office. Bring the delivery slip you received during passport enrolment. Authorized representatives (who have to bring their own NID card) can collect the applicant's new passport.
