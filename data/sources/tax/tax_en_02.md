The homepage of the National Board of Revenue (NBR), Bangladesh shows these contact numbers: 333 for the Income Tax contact center, 16555 for the VAT online contact center, and 09643717171 as the e-Return hotline number.

The homepage has a search box where a 13-digit BIN (Business Identification Number) can be searched to get basic information about the taxpayer.

The homepage also has a link for solving problems related to e-payment of tax and VAT, and an Information Library section that includes Income Tax Paripatra (circulars).
