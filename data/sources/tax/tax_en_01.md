The National Board of Revenue (NBR) portal page is titled "Welcome to Taxpayer's Identification Number (TIN) Registration / Cancellation". The page menu has these options: Home, Login, Register, Forgot Password, Third Party TIN Verification and Return Verify.

The page warns that any fraudulent use of the site is punishable under the Income Tax Act, 2023, the Information and Communication Technology Act, 2006 or any other law prevailing in Bangladesh.

For eReturn queries or support, the page lists the phone number 09643717171 and the email info@incometax.gov.bd.

This page does not itself list the steps or documents for TIN registration.
