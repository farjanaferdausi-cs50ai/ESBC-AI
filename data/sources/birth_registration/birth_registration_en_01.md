This is an official notice of the Tarundia Union Digital Centre, published on 7 September 2024. The notice says that all the required documents must be brought when applying for birth registration, that the application is not acceptable without the listed documents, and that the originals of the attached documents must be shown when the application is submitted.

If the age is 0 to 45 days, the following are needed:
1. EPI (vaccination) card.
2. The parents' online birth registration together with their national ID cards.
3. House holding number and the current year's chowkidari tax receipt.
4. Mobile number of the applicant or guardian.
5. One coloured passport-size photo with the form.

If the age is 46 days to 5 years, the following are needed:
1. EPI (vaccination) card, or a health worker's attestation on letterhead with signature and seal.
2. The parents' online birth registration together with their national ID cards.
3. If needed, the school's documents with the head teacher's attestation.
4. House holding number and the current year's chowkidari tax receipt.
5. Mobile number of the applicant or guardian.
6. One coloured passport-size photo with the form.

If the age is more than 5 years, the following are needed:
1. Educational certificate (PSC, JSC or SSC). If there is none, an attestation certificate with signature and seal from an MBBS doctor of a government hospital, and signature and seal in column 1 of item 7 of the birth registration application form are mandatory.
2. For those born after 1 January 2001, the parents' online birth registration together with their national ID cards is mandatory.
3. For those born before 1 January 2001, the national ID card is mandatory. If the parents are dead, a death certificate is mandatory.
4. For those born after 1 January 2001 whose parents are dead, the online birth registration must be taken first, then the online death registration certificate, and both certificates are submitted with the application.
5. House holding number and the current year's chowkidari tax receipt.
6. Mobile number of the applicant or guardian.
7. One coloured passport-size photo with the form.
8. The attached documents must carry the signature and seal of the head teacher of a government school or a Union Parishad member.
