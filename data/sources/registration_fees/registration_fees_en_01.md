This is an English translation, prepared by the project author, of the official Bengali page at the URL above. The Bengali page is the authoritative text.

The page was last updated on 4 April 2026. It is the official fee list of the Office of the Registrar General, Birth and Death Registration (Local Government Division). The page has separate fee PDFs for registration offices in Bangladesh and for Bangladesh missions abroad.

Fees for birth and death registration (registration offices in Bangladesh):

1. Registering a birth or death within 45 days: free of charge.

2. Registering a birth or death from 45 days after the event up to 5 years (lump sum): 25 taka.

3. Registering a birth or death more than 5 years after the event (lump sum): 50 taka.

4. Application fee to correct the date of birth: 100 taka.

5. Application fee to correct other information such as name, father's name, mother's name or address (not the date of birth): 50 taka.

6. A copy of the original certificate, or of the certificate after correction, in both Bangla and English: free of charge.

7. A duplicate copy of the certificate in both Bangla and English: 50 taka.
