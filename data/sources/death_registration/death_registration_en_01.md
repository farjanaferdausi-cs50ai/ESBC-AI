This is an English translation, prepared by the project author, of the official Bengali page at the URL above. The Bengali page is the authoritative text. It summarises the information on the page.

For online death registration the page tells applicants to go to bdris.gov.bd/dr/application. After the website opens, the application has 6 steps.

After the applicant details are filled in, the documents the page says to attach are: an attestation letter from the imam who led the funeral prayer, or from the priest who performed the cremation; the death certificate issued by the hospital; the post-mortem papers if the death was unnatural; and an attestation about the death from the local Union Parishad, Pourashava, or the ward councillor of the City Corporation. The page also mentions that all the documents should be attested.

After applying, a copy of the application must be submitted to the Union Parishad, Pourashava or City Corporation under the Local Government Division. After submission they prepare the death registration certificate from the application and give it to the applicant.

The page warns that the information in the application must be checked carefully, because correcting a mistake after the certificate is issued is troublesome.
