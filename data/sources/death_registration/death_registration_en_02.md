This is an English translation, prepared by the project author, of the official Bengali page at the URL above. The Bengali page is the authoritative text.

The page gives orgbdr.gov.bd as the website of the Office of the Registrar General, Birth and Death Registration.

The page states that a person's death registration should be completed within 45 days of the death. In the same way it states that a child's birth registration should be completed within 45 days of the birth.
